Researcher Profile

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Mechanical Engineering at Texas A&M University (anticipated July 2027), advised by a multidisciplinary committee spanning mechanical engineering and AI.

My dissertation direction is Graph-Based Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Geometry-Aware Computational Fluid Dynamics of Convection-Dominated and Turbulent Flows.

Research interests: scientific machine learning, multiphysics simulation, CFD, graph neural networks, heat transfer, reactive porous media, and energy systems.
