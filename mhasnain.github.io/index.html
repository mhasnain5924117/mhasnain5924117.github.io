My work integrates finite-volume modeling, geometry-aware deep learning, and high-impact engineering applications. I develop physically grounded computational frameworks that are scalable and generalizable for modern energy and thermal-fluid systems.

I specialize in graph-based deep learning and high-fidelity CFD, with applications in fluid flow, heat transfer, and transport in reactive porous media for energy conversion and storage.

Research Focus and PhD Direction

I am a PhD researcher in Mechanical Engineering at Texas A&M University (anticipated July 2027). My current thesis focus is: Graph-Based Deep Learning for Geometry-Aware Neural Operators for CFD & Heat Transfer Applications.

I previously developed advanced models for coupled heat and mass transfer in metal-hydride hydrogen systems, fire-exposed construction materials, and battery thermal safety, and now extend this background into scientific machine learning for robust surrogate modeling.

Funding & Opportunity Thread
